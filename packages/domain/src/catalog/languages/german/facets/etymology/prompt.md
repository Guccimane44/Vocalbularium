Name each language once, without a middle dot, and never write "stammt aus": the card adds it. For a selection in another language, the text names its earlier forms as they were written. A proper name, such as a person or a brand, has no Etymology. Never invent an etymology or guess at one: leave it out when the dictionary has none or it is unknown.
