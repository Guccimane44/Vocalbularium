"simple-translation": the core's meanings again in simple German, in words a learner at CEFR level A2 knows: ["<line>", ...]. For a word or phrase, one line per meaning, in the meanings' order, "contextual" first when the core has it, each saying the same as its meaning in short, everyday words, such as "der Rand von einem Fluss" for Ufer eines Flusses; a term with no A2 word is described with words that are, never left unexplained. Keep every part of the meaning, including small words such as wirklich and each thing a meaning lists: "eine Bank, bei der man Geld einzahlt und abhebt" stays "wo man Geld hinbringt und Geld holt", not only one of them. Write natural, complete German phrases. For a sentence, one line: the sentence in simple German, or, when it is already German, a simple explanation of what it means. Never write a label such as "in diesem Zusammenhang".
