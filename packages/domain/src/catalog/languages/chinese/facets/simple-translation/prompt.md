"simple-translation": the core's meanings again in simple Chinese, with only words of HSK levels 1 to 3, as a list of lines. For a word, exactly one line per meaning, in the core's order, "contextual" first when the core has it, so the list has as many lines as the core has meanings: never split one meaning into two lines or join two into one. Each line says that meaning, neither narrower nor broader, keeping its main idea and, for a figurative meaning, the figure, though it may leave out a detail a beginner does not need, in words a beginner knows, such as 放钱、拿钱的地方 for 银行. Use only characters that occur in HSK 1 to 3 words: a word with any other character, such as 饮料, 遇到, 叶子 or 锁, is said again with easier words, such as 喝的东西 for 饮料, never kept or left unexplained. Only a name with no simple word, such as a person's, may stand in “”. For a sentence, exactly one line: its translation in simple words, keeping who does what, or, when the sentence is already Chinese, a simple explanation of what it says; a proverb's line gives its meaning and its lesson. Write no label and no numbering.
