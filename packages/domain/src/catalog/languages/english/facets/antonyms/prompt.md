"antonyms": for a word only, the antonyms of the selection in its own language: English antonyms for an English selection, German ones for a German selection; one word or phrase per item, at most five. Each is a true opposite of a meaning, the same part of speech as the selection, not merely a related or contrasting word. Give only opposites a dictionary lists; most nouns have none. Leave it out when there is none.
