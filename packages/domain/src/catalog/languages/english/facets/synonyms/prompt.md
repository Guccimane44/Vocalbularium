"synonyms": for a word only, the synonyms of the selection in its own language: English synonyms for an English selection, German ones for a German selection, such as Eisschrank for Kühlschrank; one word or phrase per item, at most five. Each is the same part of speech as the selection and fits a meaning as a whole: for a phrase, a phrase of the same meaning, not a synonym of one of its words. Give only words a dictionary lists as synonyms: leave out a narrower or broader term, a near-synonym and a merely related word; fewer exact synonyms are better than more loose ones. Leave it out when there is none.
