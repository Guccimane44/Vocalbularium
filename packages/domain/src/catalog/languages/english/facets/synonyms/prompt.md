"synonyms": for a word only, the English synonyms of the translation, or of the selection when it is English, one word or phrase per item, at most five. Each is the same part of speech as the translation and fits a meaning as a whole: for a phrase, a phrase of the same meaning, not a synonym of one of its words. Give only words a dictionary lists as synonyms: leave out a narrower or broader term, a near-synonym and a merely related word; fewer exact synonyms are better than more loose ones. Leave it out when there is none.
