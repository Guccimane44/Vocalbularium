"simple-translation": the core's meanings again in simple American English, in words a learner at CEFR level A2 knows: ["<line>", ...]. For a word or phrase, one line per meaning, in the meanings' order, "contextual" first when the core has it, each saying the same as its meaning in short, everyday words, such as "the land next to a river" for the side of a river; a term with no A2 word is described with words that are, never left unexplained. For a sentence, one line: the sentence in simple English, or, when it is already English, a simple explanation of what it means. Never write a label such as "in this context".
