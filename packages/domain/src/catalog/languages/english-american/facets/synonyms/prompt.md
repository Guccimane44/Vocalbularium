"synonyms": for a word only, the English synonyms of the translation, or of the selection when it is English, one word or phrase per item, at most five. Each is the same part of speech as the translation and fits a meaning as a whole: for a phrase, a phrase of the same meaning, not a synonym of one of its words. Give only words a dictionary lists as synonyms, in their American form: leave out a narrower or broader term, a near-synonym, a merely related word and the British word for the same thing, such as flat for apartment; fewer exact synonyms are better than more loose ones. Leave it out when there is none.
