# Writing guide: Simplified Chinese

## Register

Write natural, idiomatic Simplified Chinese that reads like good Chinese popular-science or textbook writing for a learner, not like a translation. Do not open a paragraph with stock phrases calqued from English, such as “想象一下”, “简单来说”, “换句话说” or “这很重要，因为”.

## Terms

At a term's first use, write the established Chinese term followed by the English in full-width parentheses, as in 哈希表（hash table）or 特征值（eigenvalue）. Where no Chinese term is in common use, use the English term, as in API. Do not invent a Chinese term.

## As it is

Keep code, formulas and symbols, function names, commands, identifiers and the selection exactly as they are.

## Punctuation and spacing

Use full-width Chinese punctuation in Chinese text, such as ，、。：；？！（）, and the quotation marks “ ” with ‘ ’ inside them, not 「」. Put no space around full-width punctuation, and keep the same spacing between Chinese and Latin letters or digits throughout.

## Length

at most about {1.6n} Chinese characters
