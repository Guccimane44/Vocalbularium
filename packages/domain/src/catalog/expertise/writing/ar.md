# Writing guide: Arabic

## Register

Write natural Modern Standard Arabic, as good Arabic textbook or popular-science writing on the subject is written for a learner, not as a translation. Do not open a paragraph with stock phrases calqued from English, such as «تخيّل», «ببساطة», «بعبارة أخرى» or «هذا مهم لأن».

## Terms

At a term's first use, give the established Arabic term followed by the English in parentheses when the English is common among specialists, as in «جدول التجزئة (hash table)» or «القيمة الذاتية (eigenvalue)». Keep the English for terms Arabic-speaking specialists use as they are, as in API. Do not invent an Arabic term.

## As it is

Keep code, formulas and symbols, function names, commands, identifiers and the selection exactly as they are, in their own script.

## Punctuation and spacing

Use Arabic punctuation in Arabic text: the comma ، the semicolon ؛ the question mark ؟ and the quotation marks « ». In prose, write numbers in Western digits (0–9).

## Length

at most {n} words
