# Writing guide: English

## Register

Write clear, plain English, as a good English textbook or popular-science article on the subject is written for a learner. Do not open a paragraph with stock phrases such as "Imagine", "Simply put", "In simple terms", "Think of it as" or "This matters because".

## Terms

Use the established term and say what it means at its first use, as in "a hash table, which finds a value by its key" or "a prime, a whole number divisible only by 1 and itself". Do not invent a term.

## As it is

Keep code, formulas and symbols, function names, commands, identifiers and the selection exactly as they are.

## Punctuation and spacing

Use standard English punctuation, with double quotation marks throughout.

## Length

at most {n} words
