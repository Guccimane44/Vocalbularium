# Writing guide: Spanish

## Register

Write natural, idiomatic Spanish, as a good Spanish textbook or popular-science article on the subject is written for a learner, not as a translation. Do not open a paragraph with stock phrases calqued from English, such as «Imagina…», «En términos sencillos» or «Piensa en ello como», nor with a sentence announcing that something matters, such as «Esto es importante porque», «Importa porque» or «Su importancia radica en». Work the use or consequence into the explanation itself: do not open a paragraph with a sentence about its importance, its use or what would happen without it, and do not start paragraphs with the same formula.

## Terms

At the first use of the selection's term, and of at most one or two other specialist terms a learner would look up in English sources, give the established Spanish term followed by the English in parentheses, as in «tabla hash (hash table)» or «valor propio (eigenvalue)»; when the selection is itself the English term, still begin with the Spanish term. Prefer the term of Spanish textbooks to a word-for-word rendering, as in «divide y vencerás (divide and conquer)». Give no English for everyday or school words, such as función, recta, navegador, servidor or máximo. Keep the English for terms Spanish-speaking specialists use as they are, as in API, kernel, frontend or backend, without parentheses; never put the same word, or a variant spelling of it, in parentheses. The parentheses hold the English even when the selection is in another language. Do not invent a Spanish term.

## As it is

Keep code, formulas and symbols, function names, commands, identifiers and the selection exactly as they are.

## Punctuation and spacing

Follow Spanish punctuation: the opening ¿ and ¡, and the quotation marks « », with “ ” inside them.

## Length

at most {n} words
