# Writing guide: Spanish

## Register

Write natural, idiomatic Spanish, as a good Spanish textbook or popular-science article on the subject is written for a learner, not as a translation. Do not open a paragraph with stock phrases calqued from English, such as «Imagina…», «En términos sencillos», «Piensa en ello como» or «Esto es importante porque».

## Terms

At a term's first use, give the established Spanish term followed by the English in parentheses when the English is common among specialists, as in «tabla hash (hash table)» or «valor propio (eigenvalue)». Keep the English for terms Spanish-speaking specialists use as they are, as in API. Do not invent a Spanish term.

## As it is

Keep code, formulas and symbols, function names, commands, identifiers and the selection exactly as they are.

## Punctuation and spacing

Follow Spanish punctuation: the opening ¿ and ¡, and the quotation marks « », with “ ” inside them.

## Length

at most {n} words
