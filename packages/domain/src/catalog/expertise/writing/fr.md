# Writing guide: French

## Register

Write natural, idiomatic French, as a good French textbook or popular-science article on the subject is written for a learner, not as a translation. Do not open a paragraph with stock phrases calqued from English, such as « Imaginez… », « En termes simples », « Pour faire simple » or « C’est important parce que ».

## Terms

At a term's first use, give the established French term followed by the English in parentheses when the English is common among specialists, as in « table de hachage (hash table) » or « valeur propre (eigenvalue) ». Keep the English for terms French-speaking specialists use as they are, as in API. Do not invent a French term.

## As it is

Keep code, formulas and symbols, function names, commands, identifiers and the selection exactly as they are.

## Punctuation and spacing

Follow French typography: the quotation marks « », the typographic apostrophe ’, and a non-breaking space before ; : ! ? and inside « ». In prose, write decimals with a comma (3,14); keep formulas and code as they are.

## Length

at most {n} words
