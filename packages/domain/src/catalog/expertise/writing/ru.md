# Writing guide: Russian

## Register

Write natural, idiomatic Russian, as a good Russian textbook or popular-science article on the subject is written for a learner, not as a translation. Do not open a paragraph with stock phrases calqued from English, such as «Представьте себе», «Проще говоря», «Давайте разберёмся» or «Это важно, потому что».

## Terms

At a term's first use, give the established Russian term followed by the English in parentheses when the English is common among specialists, as in «хеш-таблица (hash table)» or «собственное значение (eigenvalue)». Keep the English for terms Russian-speaking specialists use as they are, as in API. Do not invent a Russian term.

## As it is

Keep code, formulas and symbols, function names, commands, identifiers and the selection exactly as they are.

## Punctuation and spacing

Follow Russian typography: the quotation marks « », with „ “ inside them, and a dash — between words, with a non-breaking space before it. In prose, write decimals with a comma (3,14); keep formulas and code as they are.

## Length

at most {n} words
