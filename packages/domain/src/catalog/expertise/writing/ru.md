# Writing guide: Russian

## Register

Write natural, idiomatic Russian, as a good Russian textbook or popular-science article on the subject is written for a learner, not as a translation. Do not open a paragraph with stock phrases calqued from English, such as «Представьте себе», «Проще говоря» or «Давайте разберёмся», nor with a sentence announcing that something matters, such as «Это важно, потому что», «X важен, потому что» or «Смысл в том, что». Work the use or consequence into the explanation itself: do not open a paragraph with a sentence about its importance, its use or what would happen without it, and do not start paragraphs with the same formula.

## Terms

At the first use of the selection's term, and of at most one or two other specialist terms a learner would look up in English sources, give the established Russian term followed by the English in parentheses, as in «хеш-таблица (hash table)» or «собственное значение (eigenvalue)»; when the selection is itself the English term, begin with the Russian term and give the English once. Give no English for the subject's ordinary or school words, such as матрица, процесс, прямая or максимум, or where it is the same word, as in браузер. Prefer the term of Russian textbooks to a word-for-word rendering of the English, as in «умножение дистрибутивно относительно сложения», not «распределяется по сложению». Keep the English for terms Russian-speaking specialists use as they are, as in API. The parentheses hold the English even when the selection is in another language. Do not invent a Russian term.

## As it is

Keep code, formulas and symbols, function names, commands, identifiers and the selection exactly as they are. Write a formula you add in its usual notation (x²), not x^2.

## Punctuation and spacing

Follow Russian typography: the quotation marks « », with „ “ inside them, a dash — between words, with a non-breaking space before it, and the ellipsis as one character (…). In prose, write decimals with a comma (3,14); keep formulas and code as they are.
