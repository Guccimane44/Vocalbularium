## Decision

**A facet window in two groups; a compact account menu at the sidebar's foot; decks above the recent captures.** The facet window (D13) becomes a card on the surface: a heading naming the parent with a close, then **On this card**, the parent's facets in a pale family tint, each with its › and a remove (×), and **Add**, the others with an outlined mark and a + in the family colour, the › shown on pointing or focus; a facet moves between the groups; on a page a closing line says added facets fill in on the next Retry; the question on removing a facet with content stands on a line beneath its row. Its checkboxes become Add and Remove buttons. The account menu moves from the header to the foot of the deck sidebar: the trigger shows the account's initial, name and server, and the menu opens upward over it, as wide as it, with Appearance as three icons on one row (a dropdown before, also on the sign-in view), the Language row, Diagnostic logs and Log out with icons; choosing an appearance leaves the menu open. The API's account summary gains `username` (always `admin`); an older API sends none and the trigger reads Account. The dashboard shows the deck cards first and Recent captures below them. No stored data or migration.
