## Decision

The parent editor shows **no markdown**: it no longer formats as markdown is typed, and characters such as `**`, `*`, `-`, `1.`, `` ` `` and `==` stay exactly as typed (stored escaped, shown literally on the card). Formatting comes from a **toolbar** with bold, italic, highlight, inline code, bulleted list and numbered list, each showing whether it applies at the cursor, plus Cmd/Ctrl+B and +I. The toolbar appears beside the focused part: on a facet's rule beside its tab, or on the core's top line; one at a time. No font or size choice; storage stays markdown per facet ([D09](../traceability-map.md#entries)). Each facet with content gains its own **Edit** beside its **Retry** and **Delete**: it edits that facet alone, in place, with its own toolbar, **Save** and **Cancel**, the rest of the card unchanged; it counts as editing that card ([D04](../traceability-map.md#entries)'s one card at a time), with drafts, the leave warning and save recovery as for a card. The card's **Edit** keeps editing the core and every facet together. Released as 0.7.5; 0.7.4 accepted as built at `8fa0caa`. Supersedes D09's live formatting in the editor (C22). *Amended 17 October 2026, before the owner accepted 0.7.5:* the editor looks as the card will (each part in its card styling, such as the number column and the Examples' display font); the core gets its own **Edit** and **Retry** on its top line, placed as a facet's, with Edit editing the core alone and Retry regenerating only the meanings or translation while the facets and the top line stay; the card's Edit stays, so empty facets can still be filled. Still 0.7.5. *Amended again 17 October 2026, before the owner accepted 0.7.5:* the Examples render as plain text like every other facet (no display font or size of their own); the toolbar gains a text size (Small, Normal, Large) and a font (Text, Display, Mono) for the selected text, stored inside the markdown as a bracketed span (`[text]{size=large font=display}`), so nothing stored changes and there is no migration. Still 0.7.5. *Amended a third time 17 October 2026, before the owner accepted 0.7.5:* the default highlight takes the main key's yellow, so the two match; the toolbar's Highlight applies the last colour used, with an arrow opening Yellow, Green, Blue, Pink and Orange swatches and No highlight; a colour other than yellow is stored in the bracketed span (`[text]{highlight=green}`), yellow staying `==text==`. Still 0.7.5. *Amended a fourth time 17 October 2026, before the owner accepted 0.7.5:* the main key's emphasis becomes a stored highlight written when content is generated, so the editor shows it and the card draws none of its own (existing content converted once); Context and Text are edited with the same toolbar editor, with Edit and Delete on a top row (their text converted once to markdown, reading as before); an empty parent can be edited by hand, with a top line of the main key and the language blank until a Retry. Still 0.7.5. *Amended a fifth time 17 October 2026, before the owner accepted 0.7.5:* the owner removed every core-level option: the core's own **Edit** and **Retry** (first amendment) and Context's and Text's **Edit** and **Delete** row (fourth) are gone, with the core-only Retry and save behind them; cards are edited from their tab, and facets keep their own actions. Still 0.7.5. *Amended a sixth time 18 October 2026, before the owner accepted 0.7.5:* a facet without content shows its heading in the card, dimmed, with no text, and its own **Edit**, **Retry** and **Delete**; its Edit opens its empty editor (until then it showed only as its tag, and had no Edit of its own). Still 0.7.5.
