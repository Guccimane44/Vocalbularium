## Context

The [module families](../../specs/modules.md#module-families) planned a family "for parents that write something new from the key such as a poem", added with its first parent. 0.16.4 brings three: Traditional Haiku, Modern Haiku and Joke. They write in the key's language, not the interface language (D15 does not apply), and their card has no reading of the kind Languages and Expertise cards keep.

Separately, the Expertise top line lists every flag it may carry (`hasFormula`, `hasCode`) and refuses any other. Chemistry's Equation facet needs one more, for the same reason Formula has its flag (D17): the facet applies only when the model has said the concept has one, so a facet's Retry never constructs an equation the concept does not have.

## Decision

- **A family, Creativity** (`creativity`, folder `catalog/creativity/`), for parents that write something new from the key. Its colour is indigo, `#4048a8` on white in light (tint `#e8e9f7`) and `#aab1f4` with `#151a4a` text in dark (tint `#20223a`), and its icon a sparkle, chosen by the owner on the [canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/M3h74Nd4nGppHx8wj2zXWU) on 7 November 2026 (option A). The spec's planned name "Creative" becomes "Creativity".
- **Its top line:** a new kind `{ kind: 'creativity', language, key }`. `language` is the language the card writes in, the key's, as an ISO 639-1 code or `other`, blank until generated, as Pronunciation's (D24). The card's heading names it in the interface language.
- **Its language:** a Creativity card writes in the key's language. D15's writing guides do not apply.
- **Chemistry's flag:** the Expertise top line gains an optional `hasEquation` (`yes | no`), written only for a key of the subject. Chemistry's Equation facet applies only when it is `yes`.
- **Additive only.** Every stored card still fits; no migration. `docs/api/openapi.json` is regenerated.

## Alternatives considered

- Reuse the Pronunciation top line for Creativity cards. It is the same shape today, but its kind tells the client a card is spoken; a family of its own keeps the two apart as each grows.
- An Equation facet without a flag, left out by the model when there is none. A facet's Retry has no saved flag to follow and tends to invent one: the reason D17 gave Formula its flag.
- A generic flags map on the Expertise top line. It would end the contract change per flag, but it lets any field through and drops the check of its values.

## Consequences

- A new Creativity module needs no contract change; a new Expertise flag still does.
- The family's three modules land after the canvas; Chemistry lands on this change.
