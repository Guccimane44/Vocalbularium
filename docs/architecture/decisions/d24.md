## Decision

**Pronunciation, the first audio, through a speech provider.** A **Pronunciation** module, the first in the Sound family and at most one per page, speaks the page's main key: its one text request returns one flag, `language`, the key's ISO 639-1 code or `other`, and an audio job then speaks the key as saved in that language; the card shows the language in its top line and a player, with no text of its own. A **Pronunciation** facet, last on German and Chinese, is written in the module's one request as the lines to speak (the translation per meaning, a sentence's translation, or a key already in that language), kept as markdown, and each line gets its own clip. Audio is the first heavy facet ([D17 direction](d17-one-request.md#heavy-facets-direction)): its own job after the text, in parallel, with its own loading, failure and Retry per clip. ElevenLabs is the speech provider, a second provider kind beside text, one pinned speech model; audio is never cached. Audio is stored with its component (migration `0018`) and served by the API.
