## Decision

- An account's decks keep the order their owner gives them. A deck moves by dragging its card on the dashboard or its row in the sidebar, by its body: a pointer move of more than a few pixels picks it up, and a shorter one is a click that opens the deck. From the keyboard, Space picks the focused deck up, the arrow keys move it, Space drops it and Escape puts it back; each step is announced.
- Either list follows the other at once, and a failed save puts the order back.
- A move is one mutation, `POST /api/deck/move` (`operationId`, `deckId`, `position` counted from 0), idempotent by its operation ID like every mutation. It deals the decks' existing `ordinal` values out again in the new order, so the deck list and its cursor, which page by `ordinal`, follow it.

No migration: `decks.ordinal` already ordered the decks, by creation until now, and every account keeps its current order until its owner moves a deck.

## Evidence

0.16.2: `tests/decks.test.mjs` (moves, replay, `operation_reused`, refusals, the list's pages following the order) and `tests/browser/deck-order.test.mjs` (a card dragged on Home, a sidebar row moved by keyboard and by pointer, Escape, both lists following, the order after a reload).
