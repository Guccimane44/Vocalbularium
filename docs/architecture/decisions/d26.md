## Decision

- A page component's `facets` list is in the **card's own order**, which the user changes by dragging a facet's grip, or with Space and the arrow keys. The card's sections, its tags (on the edge, folded or hanging) and its editor follow that order.
- The order belongs to one card. A deck's modules keep the parent's order, and so does every new page made from them; the facet window, the samples and the module pages keep it too.
- A facet added to a card from its facet window comes **last**; a facet deleted and added again comes last as well.
- Generation does not depend on the order: an attempt asks for the facets in the parent's order, so prompts and their snapshots are unchanged, and a Retry keeps the card's order.
- A move is one mutation, `move-facet` (`POST /api/page/facets/move`: page, component, parent, facet, a position counted from 0), idempotent by its operation ID like every mutation. It changes only the list; content, attempts and status stay as they are.

No migration: `components.facets` was already an ordered array, and a card from an earlier build keeps the parent's order as its own. The OpenAPI schema gains the route.

## Evidence

Increment 3 of the 0.16.1 plan: `tests/parents.test.mjs` and `tests/facets.test.mjs` (the move, its replay and refusals, Retry asking in the parent's order), `tests/browser/facet-order.test.mjs` (pointer and keyboard moves, Escape, the tags following, the order after a reload).
