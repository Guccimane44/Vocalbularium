## Decision

**A facet's Retry is unavailable instead of explained; a facet's About on the card; the module's ⓘ a button.** A facet the card's flags rule out shows no note; its **Retry**, on its heading and its tag, stays in place, unavailable, for every facet of every module (amends D17). Every facet's explanation shows in the facet window, without a sample where the module's sample holds none. A module's **About** below its **+** becomes a round **ⓘ** button in the **+**'s column (amends D13). An **About** first among a facet's Edit, Retry and Delete opens the facet's explanation in a dialog.
