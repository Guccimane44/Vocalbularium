## Context

The catalog's definitions carry four fields that nothing in the product reads (checked on `main` at `761bfbd`):

| Field | Set in | Read by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Module `provider` (`ProviderKind`) | the 11 content modules | nothing |
| Module `source` (`Source`) | all 14 modules | two assertions in `tests/catalog.test.mjs` that restate `kind` |
| Core `label` (always `'Core'`) | the 11 cores | nothing |
| Core and facet `output` (`OutputType`, always `'text'`) | every core and facet | one assertion in `tests/catalog.test.mjs` |

Separately, every Languages core may write a `romanization` of the key, "only when useful", which the card shows as a reading line under the key. In the Chinese module this overlaps the Pinyin facet when the key is Chinese.

## Decision

1. **Delete the four fields** and their types (`ProviderKind`, `Source`, `OutputType`) from the catalog, its type definitions, the catalog test and the documents that describe them. Nothing stored, sent or shown changes, and no prompt changes.
2. **Drop `romanization` from the Languages top line.** The card no longer shows a reading line under the key; on a Chinese card the Pinyin facet gives the reading. This one is not a cleanup: it changes the stored top line, the API contract, four prompts and what the card shows, so it is built as its own change after part 1 (see open items).

## Alternatives considered

- **Leave the fields in place.** They cost nothing at run time, but they suggest choices the product does not make (a speech provider per module, a source per module, outputs other than text) and make the schema harder to read.
- **Keep `source` for a later dictionary lookup.** If lookup comes (step 3 of the rework), the source belongs to a facet or a part of one, not to a module, so it would be a new field in any case.
- **Keep `romanization` only for keys outside the module's own Pinyin coverage.** Rejected by the owner: drop it everywhere.

## Consequences

- The module, core and facet definitions only hold fields the product uses.
- The only place that said Context is captured and Text is written by the user goes. Nothing reads it.
- After part 2, no card shows a reading under the key, whatever its script; on a Chinese card the Pinyin facet gives the reading of the Chinese text.

## Open items

1. ~~**Keys in other scripts.**~~ Resolved 10 November 2026: the owner chose "drop it everywhere". Every Languages module writes `romanization` today, for any key in a non-Latin script, and the Pinyin facet exists only in the Chinese module. After part 2, a Chinese key on a German or English card, and a Japanese or Russian key on any card, shows no reading.
2. **Upgrade of stored cards.** Stored Languages top lines may hold `romanization`, and `ParentHeaderSchema` refuses unknown fields. Either a migration strips it from stored components, or the contract keeps accepting it on read and the renderer ignores it. Upgrades keep decks and pages.
3. **When part 2 is built:** as its own PR right after part 1, or together with the flags redesign (step 2), which reshapes the top line anyway.
