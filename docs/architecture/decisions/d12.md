## Decision

Four changes proposed for 0.7.6: **empty components start folded**, a slim band under the tab offering **Unfold**, instead of only their tab; **Facet in the eject menu** of a parent on a page and in deck settings, opening a popover of ticks below the tab that apply at once, unticking a facet with content asking first; **no facets in the module window**; and **ghost tags retired**. *Rejected by the owner before acceptance:* at the time the owner was not ready for facets configured per module. The record is kept here from [PR #192](https://github.com/Guccimane44/Vocalbularium/pull/192), which was never merged; D13 revives the facet window for 0.8.1, after D11 gave each module its own facets, without the folded empty cards
