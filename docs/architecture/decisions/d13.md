## Decision

Two changes for 0.8.1. **Facets chosen on the parent.** A parent's card, and a parent in deck settings, eject **Facet** among their actions; it opens a **facet window** listing every facet of the parent's module, in the module's order, with the facets the parent already has marked as equipped. Choosing facets there replaces choosing them in the module window: the window lists no facets beneath their parent, with no drag, no **+** and no facet search, and the [ghost tags](../../specs/pages-and-editing.md#ghost-tags) that served the facet's **+** are retired. An added facet still waits, as its tag only, until the next Retry ([D08](../traceability-map.md#entries)); in deck settings the window changes the unsaved draft. The module window also drops its **Already here** mark on modules present on the page. **Explanations.** Every module and every facet gets an **explanation**, so a user can learn what it does without trying it: a few sentences on what it contributes and when to use it, with a short sample of its output for a word and for a sentence. A module's explanation opens from a link below its **+** in the module window, in a dialog; a facet's opens from an **ⓘ** on its row in the facet window, expanding beneath the row. The explanation lives in the module's or facet's catalog folder ([D11](../traceability-map.md#entries)); Claude drafts the texts and the owner reviews them. The one-line descriptions stay. Revives D12's facet window without its folded empty cards. Amends D08 (facets added from the module window) and 0.7.2's ghost tags. Nothing stored changes and no migration is expected
