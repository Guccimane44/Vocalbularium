## Decision

Entries drag by their label instead of a handle: a card by its tab's family icon and label, a deck-settings module by its icon and name. The six dots become a menu button whose entries are placeholders (*Coming soon*). A card's **Edit**, **Generate**/**Retry** and **Delete** no longer appear on hover: clicking the tab, or Enter on it, ejects them to the tab's right with a staggered spring; Space still picks the card up. A failed card keeps its **Retry** beside the tab. Amends D04's hover-and-focus actions and drag handles. *Amended 12 October 2026:* the deck-settings preview moves from a column beside the module list into a modal dialog opened by a **Preview** button above the list, so deck settings keep the page view's list-and-window layout; it no longer follows a drag as it happens (amends D05's live preview). The module window's drag instructions move behind a **?** beside its heading; the main key loses its lock mark and its **Edit** is always shown; an empty main key's "Main key can not be empty." shows inside the field; a card's actions gain **Fold**/**Unfold**, remembered per page in this browser; deleting an empty component no longer asks, and a component with content asks "Are you sure?" beside its tab instead of in a dialog, with Cancel under the pointer, and a deleted card pops where it stands; each window module gets a **+** that adds it as the last entry (reverses D04's no Add buttons), and a module being dragged in is marked in the window and in the list; dragging in from the window moves the module as an entry being reordered does; **Generate** gives way to **Retry** for every content component, and a retry that replaces content asks beside the tab like Delete, in the family colour (amends D04's Generate/Retry and the retry dialog); a generating card is only its tab with a spark running round its edge (amends D02's warning dialog); a module dragged in from the window stands in the list as the entry it will become while the entries make room
