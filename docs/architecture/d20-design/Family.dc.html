Languages

Modules that put your key into one language: they explain a word in it, or translate a sentence into it.

What every Languages module does

  1. 1
    Reads your key in any language

    It finds the key's language in the key itself, and decides whether it is a word or a sentence.

  2. 2
    Writes the core in its language

    A word's meanings, or a sentence's translation. A key already in that language is explained, not translated.

  3. 3
    Facets describe its translation

    Not your key. Each module has its own facets, in its own order, and leaves out those that do not apply.
