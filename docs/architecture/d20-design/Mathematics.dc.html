eigenvalue

An eigenvalue is a number that tells you how much a transformation stretches a special direction. Along an eigenvector a matrix only stretches a vector, by the eigenvalue.

Every square matrix has at least one eigenvalue.

For every square matrix there is at least one direction that the matrix only stretches, once complex numbers are allowed. The stretch factor along that direction is an eigenvalue.
