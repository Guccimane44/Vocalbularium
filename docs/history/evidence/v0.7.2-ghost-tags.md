# 0.7.2 Ghost tags: verification and candidate

16 October 2026. Release 0.7.2 is the owner's request in a Claude Code chat on 16 October 2026, tracked in the 0.7.2 milestone in Actual Stages. It covers:
- **Ghost tags.** With several parents that would take a facet, its **+** in the module window picks the facet up. Each of those parents wears a ghost of its tag in the slot it would take. Clicking a ghost or its card's tab places the facet; Escape, the **+** again or a click elsewhere puts it back; Tab and Shift+Tab move between the ghosts. This replaces the 0.7.0 chooser menu. With one such parent the **+** adds at once, as before.
- **Numbered modules.** Identical modules on a page or in deck settings are numbered in their tabs, such as German 1 and German 2 (requested during the same chat).

The [pages specification](../../specs/pages-and-editing.md#ghost-tags) describes the behavior.

The first 0.7.2 build, from `8842dbc` (SHA-256 `96672ae56a11140babf84dfd6c23ab767adc0c8dca3b1008ce4cb6aa4b0b3385`), was handed to the owner on 16 October 2026 and is superseded. It is kept locally as `vocabularium-0.7.2-local-candidate-8842dbc.zip`. The owner asked for two changes, made in `a13b720`:
- **No dotted outline on the cards.** Only the tab of each parent that would take the facet, and its ghost, take the click, and both glow in the family colour. A click on the rest of a card puts the facet back.
- **The tag flies in.** A facet placed by its ghost flies its tag from the facet in the module window into the ghost's place, as when added with **+** to a single parent. The ghost waits, no longer glowing, while the facet is saved.

The second build, from `a13b720` (SHA-256 `e1b036363619882aca5c03e3483256cc97799af69b427c21e99624e2cee23c6a`), is superseded too. It is kept locally as `vocabularium-0.7.2-local-candidate-a13b720.zip`. The owner found the glow too weak and the tabs and ghosts pulsing out of step. `e4743c2` makes these changes:
- **A stronger glow.** Each tab breathes an aura in its family colour, with a ring tightening round it and a sweep of light across it as the aura peaks. Each ghost swells, fills and haloes.
- **One beat.** Tabs and ghosts share a 1.6s period, and their CSS animations are set to one start time, so they stay in phase whenever they appeared.

The upgrade from 0.7.1 has no migration and keeps every deck and page ([scope](../../product/scope.md#upgrade-from-earlier-builds)). The package is not installed or accepted yet.

## Automated verification

Run locally on macOS, Node 24.21, on source revision `e4743c2`:
- PostgreSQL 18 with disposable databases.
- Playwright with its Chromium.
- Controlled generation providers: no live model calls, and not the owner's account.

Results:
- `npm run check`: passed.
- `npm test`: 153/153.
- Browser: 27/27 (`app.browser.test.mjs`, `logging.browser.test.mjs`). The owner's API held port 4318, so the suite ran against a build of the same revision for `http://127.0.0.1:4328`, not against the extracted candidate. The scenarios cover:
  - **Ghost tags with the pointer** (*parents and facets*): the **+** marks the facet and shows a ghost on each German and none on the Chinese card; the ghost stands among the tags in the parent's order (Ar, Ex, Sy, An); the **+** again and a click elsewhere put it back and add nothing; the cards have no outline, while their tabs and the ghosts glow (`glow-aura`, `ghost-aura`), all eight glow animations with one start time and one period; a click on a card's content puts the facet back; a click on the second German's tab places the facet there only, and its tag flies in from the window, starting more than 300px to the right, into the ghost's place.
  - **Ghost tags from the keyboard** (*facets from the keyboard*): Enter on the **+** focuses the first ghost; Tab and Shift+Tab cycle between the ghosts only; Escape puts the facet back and returns focus to the **+**; Enter on the second ghost places it there.
  - **Deck settings:** with two Germans, the ghosts hang below their tabs and clicking the second places the facet on it alone.
  - **Numbering:** two Germans read German 1 and German 2 on a page and in deck settings; the *deck UI* and *retry UI* scenarios now find the cards by their numbered names.
- CI on the PR.
